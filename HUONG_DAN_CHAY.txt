HUONG DAN CHAY DATABASE VA GIAO DIEN PTIT CINEMA - NHOM 30
=========================================================

Tai lieu nay huong dan khoi dong MySQL bang XAMPP, nap database va mo giao dien web.
May tinh can co XAMPP, Java 17 tro len va Maven.


1. KHOI DONG MYSQL BANG XAMPP
----------------------------
1. Mo XAMPP Control Panel.
2. Bam Start o dong MySQL va cho den khi dich vu chay.
3. Apache chi can bat neu ban muon mo phpMyAdmin qua localhost/phpmyadmin.
   Ung dung PTIT Cinema khong can Apache XAMPP de phuc vu giao dien; Spring Boot
   se phuc vu trang web tren cong 8080.


2. NAP DATABASE NEU CHUA CO HOAC MUON TAO LAI
----------------------------------------------
1. Mo trinh duyet va vao http://localhost/phpmyadmin.
2. Chon tab Nhap (Import).
3. Bam Chon tep va chon file:
   database\database.sql
   (duong dan day du: C:\_CSDL\PTIT_Cinema_Group30\PTIT_Cinema_Project\database\database.sql)
4. De dinh dang la SQL, keo xuong cuoi trang va bam Nhap/Thuc hien.
5. Cho phpMyAdmin chay xong. Kiem tra database QuanLyRapChieuPhim va cac bang
   nhu Phim, KhachHang, SuatChieu, HoaDon, Ve.
6. Import bon migration theo thu tu:
   database\migrations\V1__account_security.sql
   database\migrations\V2__customer_phone_login.sql
   database\migrations\V3__qr_transfer_payments.sql
   database\migrations\V4__schedule_management.sql
   V1 tao bang tai khoan; V2 chuyen khach sang dang nhap bang so dien thoai;
   V3 bo sung thanh toan QR, giu ghe tam va lich su ghe tren hoa don. Cac
   V4 tao bang gia ve mac dinh, cho phep admin cap nhat gia va quan ly suat
   chieu. Cac migration khong xoa database. Neu V1-V3 da chay truoc do, chi
   import V4 mot lan.

CANH BAO QUAN TRONG:
File SQL co lenh DROP DATABASE IF EXISTS QuanLyRapChieuPhim. Chay file se xoa
database QuanLyRapChieuPhim cu va tao lai tu dau. Moi du lieu da nhap trong
database nay se mat. Hay xuat/sao luu truoc neu can giu du lieu.
Neu database va du lieu mau da co san va ban khong muon lam moi, bo qua buoc Import.


3. CAU HINH KET NOI MYSQL
-------------------------
Ung dung doc cau hinh trong:
src\main\resources\application.properties

URL mac dinh ket noi toi:
localhost:3306/QuanLyRapChieuPhim

Cac bien moi truong duoc ho tro:
- DB_USERNAME: ten tai khoan MySQL (mac dinh trong ung dung la root).
- DB_PASSWORD: mat khau MySQL.
- MAIL_HOST, MAIL_PORT, MAIL_USERNAME, MAIL_PASSWORD, MAIL_FROM: SMTP gui thu moi nhan vien (khong can cho khach dang ky).
- APP_PUBLIC_BASE_URL: dia chi public de tao lien ket thu moi nhan vien.
- APP_BOOTSTRAP_ADMIN_EMAIL va APP_BOOTSTRAP_ADMIN_PASSWORD: tao admin lan dau.
- PAYMENT_TRANSFER_EXPIRY_MINUTES: so phut giu ghe (mac dinh 15; gioi han 1-120).
- SESSION_COOKIE_SECURE=true khi chay tren HTTPS.

Neu MySQL XAMPP cua ban dung root va mat khau trong (cau hinh thuong gap cua
XAMPP), co the giu cau hinh mac dinh. Neu ban da dat mat khau, can dat DB_PASSWORD
cho dung truoc khi chay ung dung, hoac cap nhat cau hinh local.
Khong dua mat khau that vao file cong khai, anh chup man hinh, GitHub hoac bao cao.


4. CHAY UNG DUNG SPRING BOOT
----------------------------
1. Mo thu muc du an PTIT_Cinema_Project bang VS Code.
2. Chon Terminal > New Terminal.
3. Khach dang ky bang so dien thoai nen khong can cau hinh SMTP. Neu can tao
   admin dau tien, dat cac bien admin bootstrap trong terminal (khong ghi mat
   khau that vao file cong khai), sau do chay:

   $env:APP_BOOTSTRAP_ADMIN_EMAIL = "admin@example.com"
   $env:APP_BOOTSTRAP_ADMIN_PASSWORD = "..."
   mvn spring-boot:run

Admin bootstrap chi tao tai khoan neu email chua ton tai; sau khi tao xong co the
bo hai bien APP_BOOTSTRAP_ADMIN_* khoi moi truong. Chi can SMTP neu muon admin
gui thu moi tai khoan nhan vien; khach hang khong can email de dang ky/dang nhap.

QR Agribank tinh da duoc dong goi trong ung dung, khong can khai bao cac bien
PAYMENT_BANK_* hoac PAYMENT_ACCOUNT_*. Khach phai tu nhap dung so tien va ma
tham chieu cua don vi QR tinh khong tu dien hai thong tin nay. He thong khong
tu nhan thong bao tu ngan hang; admin phai doi chieu giao dich trong ung dung
ngan hang truoc khi bam xac nhan. Don het han se tu dong nha ghe; neu tien den
muon, can xu ly/hoan tien thu cong.

Neu terminal chua dung o thu muc du an, chay:

   cd C:\TEST_CSDL\PTIT_Cinema_Group30\PTIT_Cinema_Project
   mvn spring-boot:run

4. Cho den khi terminal hien dong gan giong:

   Started CinemaApplication

Giu terminal nay mo trong luc su dung giao dien. Khong dong terminal, neu khong
ung dung Spring Boot se dung.

Giao dien quan ly Phim va Khach hang (can tai khoan STAFF hoac ADMIN):
- Tim kiem phim theo ma hoac ten.
- Tim kiem khach hang theo ma, ho ten, so dien thoai hoac email.
- Them, sua, xoa phim va khach hang.

Sau khi them/sua/xoa phim va khach hang thanh cong, danh sach tren trang se
duoc tai lai. Admin quan ly suat chieu va gia ghe tai QuanTriLichChieu.html.
Trang nay su dung cac phong va quan ly da co, hien thi so ghe, so ve/cho va so
suat trong moi phong. Suat moi phai la tuong lai va khong duoc trung gio/phong.
Khong sua/xoa suat da co ve, don hang hoac ghe duoc giu. Gia moi chi ap dung
cho don moi; hoa don cu giu nguyen gia da luu. Them phong vat ly va so do ghe
hien van can cap nhat database rieng, chua co form tren web.

Khach hang dang ky tai DangNhap.html bang so dien thoai va mat khau, khong can
xac minh email. Sau 5 lan nhap sai lien tiep, tai khoan bi khoa den khi admin mo khoa. Admin moi
nhan vien qua QuanTri.html; nhan vien dat mat khau qua email moi. Nen co it nhat
hai tai khoan ADMIN de tai khoan con lai co the mo khoa neu mot admin bi khoa.

Trang DatVe.html cho phep chon phim/suat chieu, chon ghe, them do an va tao hoa
don/ve cho tai khoan khach da dang nhap. Khach co the chuyen khoan QR neu admin
da cau hinh; ghe duoc giu theo thoi han cau hinh (mac dinh 15 phut). Admin can tu
doi chieu tien va xac nhan thu cong; he thong chua ket noi API ngan hang. Gia ghe
thuong mac dinh 100.000 dong, ghe VIP 50.000 dong; backend tu tinh lai gia.

Du lieu suat chieu mau hien su dung ngay nam 2024 de phuc vu cac truy van bao
cao. Trang dat ve hien thi dung lich trong database, ke ca ngay da qua. Hay cap
nhat lich chieu truoc khi su dung cho hoat dong thuc te.


6. DUNG UNG DUNG
----------------
Quay lai terminal VS Code dang chay ung dung va nhan Ctrl+C.
Thao tac nay chi dung Spring Boot, khong xoa database.

Neu khong con dung phpMyAdmin hoac ung dung, co the bam Stop cho MySQL trong
XAMPP Control Panel. Database van duoc luu tren may va co the mo lai lan sau.


7. LOI THUONG GAP
-----------------
- "Communications link failure" hoac khong ket noi MySQL:
  Kiem tra MySQL trong XAMPP da Start chua, cong co phai 3306 khong va ten
  database co dung QuanLyRapChieuPhim khong.

- "Access denied for user":
  Kiem tra DB_USERNAME va DB_PASSWORD co trung voi tai khoan MySQL tren XAMPP
  khong. Khong gui mat khau len chat/cong khai.

- "Unknown database":
  Import file database/database.sql truoc, hoac kiem tra database trong
  phpMyAdmin co ten QuanLyRapChieuPhim.

- "Port 8080 was already in use":
  Ung dung khac dang dung cong 8080. Dung ung dung cu bang Ctrl+C hoac doi
  server.port trong application.properties.

- Lenh "mvn" khong duoc nhan dien:
  Kiem tra Maven da duoc cai dat va co trong PATH; sau do dong/mo lai terminal.
